FAQ
What is TRASHCORE?
A fast, anonymous media host. Upload images, video, or audio and get a permanent public link instantly. No account needed.
How large can my files be?
Up to 50MB per file, up to 10 files per batch.
How do I delete my file?
Save the delete token you get after uploading, then use "Delete file" in the top bar.
Are uploads private?
No — anyone with the link can view the file. Don't upload private or sensitive content.
How long are files stored?
Indefinitely, in general — but we may remove files at any time, especially ones that break the rules.
Is my IP logged?
Yes, for abuse prevention only. It's not sold or shared except when legally required.
How do I report abuse?
Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram (links in the top bar) with the file URL and reason.
